Frequently Asked Questions
How do I enroll in a course? ▾
Browse courses from the Courses page, filter by category or level, and click "Enroll Now" on any open course. You'll get instant access to all materials.
Can I access courses on mobile? ▾
Absolutely! EduLMS is fully responsive and works seamlessly on any device — phones, tablets, and desktops.
How are assignments graded? ▾
Instructors manually grade written assignments with rubric-based feedback. Quizzes and MCQ tests are auto-graded with instant results.
Is my data secure? ▾
Yes. All data is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS, the platform is GDPR & FERPA compliant, and student records are retained for 7 years per educational regulations.
How do I contact support? ▾
Visit our Contact page to send a message, or email support@edulms.edu. Our team responds within 24 hours on business days.
